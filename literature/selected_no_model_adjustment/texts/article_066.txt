I.2   Memorization-Independent Battles among
      LLMs (MIB)
To overcome the shortcomings of the FRMM
method, we referred to the pairwise battle method
adopted in LLM evaluation (Zheng et al., 2023). By
tallying each LLM’s performance in a “round-robin”
tournament of pairwise match-ups, we ranked the
performance of the LLMs.
   Specifically, we selected two LLMs at each time
and filtered the MRI task’s reasoning questions
based on the performance of these two LLMs in
memorization tasks. We only retained the reasoning
questions whose related memorization questions
were correctly answered by both LLMs. In this way,
the two LLMs are battled under fair conditions. We
