H.1    Models

We use commercially available models from two of the most popular providers: Google and OpenAI.
To measure the effects of compression and computational constraints, we evaluate models at different
distillation sizes and ablate the effect of different “thinking” or “reasoning” levels. Where possible,
we set the temperature to 0.2 to enhance reproducibility. We ensure natural experiment queries use
facts that fall inside the knowledge cutoffs. Full details are available in Table 4.
Throughout all natural experiments, Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite was used to parse and evaluate outputs
against ground truth answers.

do not mention quantization details for the smaller Gemini 3 Flash and GPT 5.4 Mini.
