    Definition 3.5 Pairwise calibration

    The pairwise calibration between two models is the Kendall’s 𝜏-b (Kendall, 1945) between their
    confidence-difference and performance-difference vectors across items, using the tie-corrected
    𝜏-b variant so that pairs the models agree on do not attenuate the correlation.

    This is pairwise calibration in the discriminatory sense (Def. 3.1). For each model pair and
each item, we ask whether model A actually outperforms model B when A says “I can solve this”
and B says “I cannot” and vice versa. A concordant pair is one where the more-confident model
performs better; a discordant pair is the reverse. Positive 𝜏 therefore means a model’s confidence
