5.3          Analysis Experiments

Ablation Study. To investigate the effectiveness of each component within ATA, we conduct ablation
studies with results shown in Table 2. i) Importance of structure information: The variants of ATA
without structure information select active token spans across the entire text, leading to a notable
performance drop. This is likely due to masking essential global context or covering mixed and
incomplete sub-tasks, highlighting the necessity of structural guidance for safe and effective attention
steering, which restricts steering spans and granularity. ii) Importance of attention masking modules:
ATA outperforms variants using only mutually exclusive or dynamic masking, and achieves superior
error reduction across all generation error types in Figure 4(a), validating their efficacy. Specifi-
cally, the mutual mask effectively prevents blending comprehension between mutually exclusive
sub-tasks, thus eliminating mixed execution and wrong decisions. The dynamic mask eliminates
interference from inactive sub-tasks during generation, ensuring faithful execution of active sub-tasks.
