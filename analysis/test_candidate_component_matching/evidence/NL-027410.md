4         Empirical Demonstrations
We apply the TEE decomposition across three empirical domains in the main text: binary safety clas-
sification (AILuminate), multiple-choice QA (MMLU), and pairwise model comparison validated
against human votes (Chatbot Arena). For the safety and Arena demonstrations, we decompose
variance across judges, treating each (prompt, SUT-response) pair as an item, while in MMLU there
is no judge and we decompose variance across SUTs scored against the answer key (SI Appendix,
Section C). Section 4.4 then quantifies the gaming surface single-configuration benchmarks expose.
In the SI, we provide a political affinity annotation demonstration (Section I.1) with a 9-annotator
human baseline, a Likert vs pairwise scoring comparison (Section H), and a pilot validation showing
that a small sample recovers the qualitative D-study guidance of a 4.5× larger full run (Section I).
